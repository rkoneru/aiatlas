# Usability review: desktop site and phone app (26 Nov 2026)

## How the review was done

I walked the site as a first-time visitor would, at two sizes:

- **Desktop:** 1280 × 800.
- **Phone:** iPhone 13, 390 × 844, touch.

Six journeys:
1. **Arrive and orient:** what is this, and where do I start?
2. **Find a topic:** menu and search.
3. **Read a concept page:** diagram, sections, detail.
4. **Use a tool:** a calculator and the proposal builder.
5. **Learn:** the course, practice and quizzes.
6. **Edge cases:** an old link, an unknown address, dark mode, the back button.

I judged each against the standard usability heuristics (Nielsen's ten), and measured what could be measured: positions on the screen, target sizes, behaviour on back and reload.

## Verdict

**Desktop:** friendly and capable, with one clear gap.
- **What works:** search is excellent, the role-based paths give newcomers a start, the diagrams are the best part of the site, and the tools give instant, plain-language results.
- **The gap:** long pages (6,000+ px) have no in-page navigation.

**Phone:** now feels like an app, with three issues that would trip up real users:
1. **Back button with a popup open:** it leaves the page instead of closing the popup.
2. **Tapping a box in a diagram:** its details open off-screen, so nothing seems to happen.
3. **Selected section tab:** it's forgotten on reload and on going back.

All three are fixable without changing the content.

**Scores** (1–5; 5 = no problems found):

| Heuristic | Desktop | Phone | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visibility of system status | 4 | 3 | Path progress, "Last reviewed" and step captions are good. On phones, a diagram tap gives no visible response (details off-screen). |
| Match with the real world | 4 | 4 | Plain-language writing and glossary tooltips. Some menu names are insider jargon: "FDE kit", "JEV vs LLM", "CAIO". |
| User control and freedom | 4 | 2 | **Phone back button leaves the page with a popup open.** Tabs aren't kept on reload or back. |
| Consistency and standards | 4 | 3 | Phone icons mix emoji (menu) and line icons (tiles, bar). The course, playground and Team mode use the older phone layout. |
| Error prevention | 4 | 4 | Calculators take numbers only. Nothing destructive without confirmation. |
| Recognition rather than recall | 3 | 4 | Desktop: 15 menu groups, nothing to say which to open first. Phone: the Explore sheet shows "You're here". |
| Flexibility and efficiency | 5 | 4 | `/` and Ctrl K search, arrow-key diagrams, tab arrow keys, Previous/Next in sheets. |
| Aesthetic and minimal design | 3 | 4 | Desktop pages are dense and long. The phone layout is clean; the home page and tool pages are still long. |
| Help users recover from errors | 5 | 5 | The 404 page suggests the closest pages and offers search. Old `#links` and merged pages redirect. Empty search says what to do. |
| Help and documentation | 4 | 4 | "Choose your path", the placement test, the glossary and "Text version of this diagram". |

## Findings, by priority

### P1: fix first (users get stuck or lost)

| # | Where | What happens | Evidence | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phone: tile popup and Explore sheet | Android's back gesture (and the back button) navigates to the previous page instead of closing the popup, so you lose your place. | Opened a tile on `/agentic-coding/`, pressed back: landed on `/rag/`. | When a sheet opens, add a history entry; back closes the sheet. The standard app behaviour. |
| 2 | Phone: diagrams | Tapping a box opens its details below the controls, off-screen: on `/rag/` the panel starts at 975 px in a 664 px viewport. People think the tap did nothing. | Measured after tapping the first box. | Show a box's details in the bottom sheet (like tiles), or scroll the panel into view. |
| 3 | Phone: section tabs | Reloading, or going back to a page, returns to the first tab. A link can't open a specific tab. | Chose tab 3 on `/agentic-coding/`, reloaded: tab 1. | Remember the tab per page for the session (`sessionStorage`), and reflect it in the URL (for example `?tab=…`) so it can be shared. |

### P2: important (friction on common journeys)

| # | Where | What happens | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Desktop: long pages | `/rag/` is 6,360 px with 9 sections and no way to jump within the page. The sidebar only lists pages. | Add a sticky "On this page" list on the right at ≥ 1200 px, or the same section tabs as phones. |
| 5 | Phone: home page | The first diagram is 1,494 px down, below four large path cards. The home page is 4,160 px tall. | Show the path choices as a compact row of four icon tiles (as in Explore). Move the course banner below the diagram. |
| 6 | Phone: tap targets | 29 of 38 controls on a typical page are under the 44 px phone guideline (Apple 44 pt, Material 48 dp). Diagram controls are 32 px tall; "Infographic" and "Read more" are 28 px. All meet the WCAG 24 px minimum. | Make the diagram controls, "Read more" and the header chips 40–44 px tall on phones. |
| 7 | Phone: tool pages | Calculators stack one input per row: `/cx-metrics/` has 10 inputs before any result, and the page is about 4,100 px. | Put inputs in two columns, show the results summary first (sticky or at the top), or open the tool full-screen from a tile. |
| 8 | Phone: course, playground, Team mode | These still use the older phone layout (no tabs, no tiles), so they feel different from the rest. | Bring the course into the app layout; the playground can stay as a desktop-first tool with a note. |
| 9 | Desktop: menu | 15 groups of equal weight, with insider names ("FDE kit", "CAIO · Chief AI Officer", "JEV vs LLM"). New visitors don't know which to open. | Put "Start here" and "Choose your path" first and visually distinct. Give jargon groups a plain subtitle (for example "FDE kit: delivery playbooks"). |

### P3: polish

| # | Where | What happens | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | Phone: icons | The Explore sheet uses emoji, which look different on each phone. Tiles and the bottom bar use line icons. | Use one line-icon set everywhere. |
| 11 | Phone: tile labels | Tile tags are 9 px uppercase, the kicker 10.5 px. Hard to read at arm's length. | 11 px minimum. |
| 12 | Phone: glossary | 15 category chips come before the first term. | Put the categories in a single scrolling row, or a "Filter" button that opens a sheet. |
| 13 | Phone: course banner | On the home page the banner's text is squeezed into a narrow column beside the icon and button. | Stack it: text on top, button full width. |
| 14 | Both: dark mode | It follows the system setting only; there's no in-page switch. | Optional: a light/dark switch in the Explore sheet and the desktop sidebar footer. |
| 15 | Both: naming | "Course" on the bottom bar vs "AI mastery course" on the page and "🎓 Course" in tools. | Use one name. |

## What already works well (keep it)

- **Search:** instant and full-text, with highlighted snippets, keyboard shortcuts and a clear empty state.
- **Starting points:** role-based paths with time estimates and saved progress; the placement test builds a personal course.
- **Diagrams:** step-through with captions, voice-over, export and a text version. On phones they're laid out vertically to fit.
- **Tools:** calculators give results instantly, in plain language, with caveats ("this is a gross figure…").
- **Phone navigation:** the bottom bar and the Explore sheet ("You're here", topics, find a page).
- **Recovery:** the helpful 404 page, old links redirect, and merged pages land on the right section.
- **Speed:** a 56 ms worst key press in glossary search on a phone-class CPU, Lighthouse 85–97 on mobile, no layout shift.
- **Accessibility:** axe reports 0 violations on all 103 pages, light and dark, desktop and phone.

## Suggested order

1. **P1 #1–#3** (back button, diagram details, tab memory): small, contained changes in `src/scripts/mobile.ts`, high impact.
2. **P2 #5 and #6** (compact home, bigger targets): CSS and the inline layout script.
3. **P2 #4** (desktop "On this page"): reuses the tab code.
4. **P2 #7–#9** (tool pages, course layout, menu labels).
5. **P3 polish.**
