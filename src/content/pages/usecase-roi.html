How to read the quadrants

Quick wins

High value, high feasibility. Fund now, ship in weeks, use them to build credibility and the platform.

Big bets

High value, harder to deliver. Fund in stages with clear go/no-go gates and a senior sponsor.

Fill-ins

Easy but modest value. Good for teams learning the platform, or bundle into a bigger initiative.

Money pits

Hard and low value. Park them, or redesign the use case until it moves into another quadrant.
