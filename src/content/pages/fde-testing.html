Regression testing for conversations

A conversational agent changes whenever its prompt, model, knowledge or tools change. Regression testing catches what broke: a golden set of real conversations with expected outcomes, simulated users that probe edge cases, and coverage across languages and channels. Run it on every change, and block release when it fails.

Plan your test suite

What to assert

Outcome

Did the conversation reach the right end state: resolved, correct handoff, correct tool call with correct arguments?

Content

Is the answer grounded in the right source, complete and free of prohibited statements? Use code checks first, an LLM judge second.

Experience

Turns to resolution, tone, length per channel, latency per turn, and whether the customer had to repeat themselves.

Simulated users are another LLM playing a customer persona with a goal and a hidden fact. Vary patience, clarity, language and mood. Review a sample by hand: simulated customers are often more cooperative than real ones.
