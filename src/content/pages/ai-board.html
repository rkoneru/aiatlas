Risk appetite statements

Low appetite

Fully automated decisions that affect people's rights (hiring, credit, health); unreviewed external communications in regulated areas; any use that breaches law or licensing.

Moderate appetite

Customer-facing assistants with human escalation; agents taking reversible actions under spend and scope limits; vendor models with contractual data protections.

High appetite

Internal productivity tools, drafting and summarization with human review, experimentation in sandboxes with synthetic or approved data.

Escalate to the board

Material incidents, new high-risk use cases, major vendor dependencies, and regulatory findings.
