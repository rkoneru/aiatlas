The Chief AI Officer playbook

A Chief AI Officer turns AI from scattered experiments into a managed portfolio that pays back. The job has six parts: set the strategy, pick and fund the right use cases, build the shared platform, govern the risk, grow the talent and adoption, and prove the value to the board. Step through the operating loop, then use the tools in this section to do each part.

The mandate

1 · Strategy

Tie AI to three to five business outcomes, choose where to differentiate, and decide build, buy or partner.

2 · Portfolio

Run one intake and scoring process for every use case. Fund in stages; kill what misses its metrics.

3 · Platform & data

One model gateway, RAG and agent runtime, eval and observability stack, and governed data access for every team.

4 · Governance & risk

Policy, risk tiers, approvals, compliance (EU AI Act, state laws), incident response. See AI compliance.

5 · Talent & adoption

AI literacy for everyone, builders in every function, champions, and redesigned roles. See AI strategy & adoption.

6 · Value

Baselines before launch, measured outcomes after, reported in money the CFO accepts.
