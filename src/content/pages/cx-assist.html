Customer service AI · Human + AI

Agent assist & live-agent handoff

The best customer-service AI makes the human better, not absent. Two moments decide the experience: the handoff, where the virtual agent passes the customer to a person without making them repeat themselves, and assist, where AI helps that person in real time and writes up the conversation afterwards.

The context packet

What travels with the customer

  • Verified identity and authentication level
  • Intent, the customer's own words, and the summary so far
  • What the virtual agent tried and the result
  • Records looked up (order, account, case number)
  • Sentiment trend and the reason for handoff
  • Suggested next step for the human

Agent assist during the conversation

  • Knowledge surfacing: answers with the source article, not just text
  • Next-best action: the step policy suggests now
  • Compliance prompts: required disclosures, prohibited phrases
  • Form and CRM filling from the conversation
  • After-call work: summary, disposition, follow-up tasks
Measure assist by what changes for agents: after-call work time, time to proficiency for new hires, and how often suggestions are accepted. Low acceptance usually means poor knowledge, not lazy agents.
