Study smarter with AI

AI can make studying much more effective, or let you feel productive while learning nothing. The difference is whether AI does the remembering for you, or makes you practice remembering. Notes → AI summary → read it → forget is the wrong way. Learn → close notes → recall → practice → AI feedback → fix → recall again is the right one.

The AI-off test

Can you, without AI:

  • Explain the concept?
  • Solve a problem?
  • Recall the key facts?
  • Apply the idea?
  • Teach someone else?

Independence is the real metric.

AI should not

  • Complete every assignment
  • Give every answer immediately
  • Replace your textbook
  • Replace your own problem solving

Instead it should

  • Explain, quiz and challenge
  • Give hints, not answers
  • Review your work
  • Help you organize

Use it responsibly

  • Verify important information
  • Use your actual course material
  • Follow academic-integrity rules
  • Don't submit work you don't understand
  • Protect private information

Never let AI become the step between you and thinking.
