Every variant fixes a specific weakness of the one before it: bad ranking, short queries, wrong documents, missing relationships, unnecessary retrieval, or a single fixed source. Pick a variant to walk through its flow.

Compare them all

Rule of thumb: start with retrieve-and-rerank + hybrid search. Add corrective or adaptive routing when you see failures. Reach for graph or agentic RAG only when questions are multi-hop or span several systems.

RAG vs Agentic RAG vs Graph RAG

Different approaches, same goal: better answers. RAG = retrieve. Agentic RAG = plan + act. Graph RAG = think in connections. Switch between them to compare the flows.
