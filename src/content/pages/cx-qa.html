QA at scale & omnichannel continuity

Traditional QA reviews a tiny sample of conversations by hand. With transcription and an LLM judge you can score every conversation against the same rubric, then spend human time calibrating the judge and coaching. The same conversation record is what lets a customer move from chat to phone without starting over.

Build a QA rubric

Pick the criteria that matter for your operation, set weights and mark the ones that fail a conversation automatically. Export it as the judge prompt.

Omnichannel continuity

One customer

Identity resolution links phone number, email, app login and chat cookie to one profile, with consent and verification rules per channel.

One conversation

A shared conversation record (summary, open case, last step) follows the customer. A switch from chat to voice opens with "I see you were asking about…".

Channel-fit answers

The same answer is shaped per channel: short and spoken for voice, links and buttons for chat, full detail for email.
