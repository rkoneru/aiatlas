AI roles & systems design

Stop using AI only to get answers. Use it to think, test, create and solve with you. The better question isn't "What can AI answer?" but "What role should AI play in this problem?" Pick a role to get a starter prompt.

Which role fits your situation?

Better prompts→Better thinking→Better decisions→Better outcomes

Use AI as a systems designer

Don't ask AI to do the task. Ask it to design the system that makes the task easy. Tasks come and go; systems multiply your impact. Build once, benefit for a long time.

The problem with task-by-task AI

  • Starting from scratch again and again
  • Inconsistent results
  • Wasting time on repeated explanations
  • No long-term leverage

The power of AI as a systems designer

  • Designs repeatable, reliable systems and workflows
  • Saves time and mental energy
  • Creates consistency and better results
  • Builds frameworks you can reuse
  • Turns one-time help into long-term leverage

Instead of…
"Write a content plan for me."
You get one plan, once, and next week you start over.

    
Ask it to design the system
  • What's the end goal and what are the milestones?
  • What are the content pillars and topics?
  • How do I research, create, review and publish?
  • What tools and templates should I use?
  • How do I batch work and schedule content?
  • How do I measure performance and improve?
  • How can I automate or simplify parts of it?
