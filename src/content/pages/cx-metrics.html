Contact-center AI metrics & value calculator

The contact-center business case rests on a handful of metrics: containment (resolved without a person), average handle time (AHT) for what still reaches a person, first-contact resolution (FCR) and customer satisfaction (CSAT). Enter your own numbers; nothing here uses industry benchmarks you can't verify.

Definitions that hold up in a review

MetricFormulaWatch out for
Containment ratecontacts ended in self-service ÷ contacts that entered self-serviceCounts abandonment as success. Report resolved containment instead.
Resolved containmentcontained contacts with no repeat contact on the same issue within N days ÷ enteredPick N (often 3–7 days) and keep it fixed.
Average handle time(talk + hold + after-call work) ÷ handled contactsAutomation removes easy contacts, so AHT on what remains can rise even as total workload falls.
First-contact resolutionissues resolved with no repeat contact within N days ÷ issuesNeeds an issue key to link repeat contacts across channels.
CSATsatisfied responses (4–5 of 5) ÷ responsesSurvey bias: unhappy and bot-abandoning customers often skip surveys.
Transfer ratevirtual-agent conversations handed to a person ÷ virtual-agent conversationsLow isn't always good; it can mean customers are stuck.
Cost per contacttotal channel cost ÷ contactsInclude platform, LLM usage, telephony, people, QA and knowledge upkeep.
Occupancy & FTEFTE ≈ workload hours ÷ (paid hours × occupancy × (1 − shrinkage))Savings arrive through attrition and hiring avoided, not overnight.
