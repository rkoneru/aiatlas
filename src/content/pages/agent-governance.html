AI agents watching agents

Human review at the end of the chain doesn't scale; it becomes a bottleneck with a compliance label on it. The model that scales: agents check agents, every action is logged, and humans govern the system and handle the high-stakes exceptions instead of inspecting every transaction.

The five-part framework

#ClaimReality
1The single-agent assumption is already obsolete. Most enterprise implementations now use multi-agent architectures, and every handoff is a new failure point and a new chance for errors to compound.Your risk doesn't scale linearly with agents. It compounds.
2Human-in-the-loop alone won't scale. Human review works at 10 agent actions a day. It breaks at 10,000.Reserve human judgment for high-stakes decisions. Use AI to govern the routine.
3Reviewer agents operate on a different mandate. Worker agents optimize for task completion; reviewer agents optimize for accuracy, compliance and risk. It's the same principle as separation of duties in finance.Independent verification catches what execution misses.
4Audit trails make multi-agent systems defensible. Every agent action is logged: outcomes, reasoning chains and handoff states.You can't explain what you can't trace.
5Governance is now a competitive advantage. How organizations use agents is expected to shape competitive position, yet few have restructured work around them.Treat governance as an accelerator, not a brake pedal.
