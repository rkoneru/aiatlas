Diagram playground

Design your own architecture diagram in the same style as the rest of the Atlas. Add boxes and connections, drag boxes to arrange them, and the playground writes the steps and the sequence diagram for you. Every diagram gets the same Play, step-through, Diagram/Sequence toggle and ⤓ Export menu (GIF, PNG, SVG, interactive HTML, Mermaid, JSON). Your work is saved in this browser.

One connection per line: User -> Agent : asks a question. Use --> for a dashed line and <-> for two-way. Optional first line: title: My diagram. You can also paste a JSON file exported from any Atlas diagram.

How to use
  1. Pick a template or import text above, or add boxes in the Boxes tab.
  2. Drag boxes in the preview to arrange them.
  3. Turn on Connect mode, then click two boxes to connect them.
  4. Click any box or connection to edit it here.
  5. Steps and the sequence diagram are generated automatically; switch to manual in Steps to write your own.

Steps follow the connections outward from the starting boxes. Each step becomes a band in the sequence diagram.
