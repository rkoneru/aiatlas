Latency budget

Gaps in human conversation are short. If the agent's first audio arrives too late, callers talk over it or hang up. Tune each stage to see the time to first audio.

  • Stream everything: partial transcripts, streamed LLM tokens, streamed TTS.
  • Speak a short acknowledgement ("Let me check that…") while a slow tool runs.
  • Keep the model and voice servers in the caller's region, and cap tool timeouts with a fallback line.
