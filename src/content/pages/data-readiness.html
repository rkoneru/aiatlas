Architecture · Before you build

Data readiness assessment

Most AI projects that slip, slip on data, not models. Before choosing an architecture, check every data source the use case needs on six questions: can we reach it, is it accurate, is it complete and current, who owns it and who may see it, may we legally use it this way, and do we have real examples to test against. Score each source below; the result tells you what is ready, what can be fixed in weeks, and what blocks the project.
