Evaluate the revision

One good answer is not proof. Run the original and revised prompt on the same 3–5 representative inputs, including at least one difficult case, and score each criterion from 1 (poor) to 5 (strong).

Prompting is not fact-checking

AI can sound confident and still be wrong. Asking it to be accurate doesn't guarantee accuracy.

  1. Check important facts, names, dates, numbers and links.
  2. Trace claims to reliable source material.
  3. Confirm that quotations and citations actually support the statements.
  4. Use human judgment for consequential or specialized decisions.

Before you send the prompt

Don't just ask AI what to do. Tell it what good looks like. Prompt → review → refine → verify. Use AI as an assistant, not an unquestioned authority.
