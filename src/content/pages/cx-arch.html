Customer service AI · Architecture

Contact-center AI reference architecture

Customer service is where most enterprises meet AI at scale. A modern contact center puts an AI layer across every channel: a virtual agent resolves what it safely can, hands off to a person with full context when it can't, assists the human during the conversation, and analyzes 100% of conversations afterwards. Step through one customer journey below.

The layers

📞 Channels

Voice (phone, IVR), web and in-app chat, messaging (SMS, WhatsApp), email and social. Each has different latency, length and compliance rules, but they should share one conversation record.

🔀 Routing & identity

Identify and verify the customer, detect intent and urgency, and decide: virtual agent, a specific queue, or a priority human. Routing uses the customer's history, not just the words of the first message.

🤖 Virtual agent

An LLM with retrieval over policies and knowledge, tools for the customer's own data (orders, bookings, balances), and deterministic flows where steps must happen exactly (authentication, payments, disclosures).

🤝 Handoff

Escalation with a context packet: who the customer is, what they asked, what was tried, sentiment, and the suggested next step. The customer never repeats themselves. See assist & handoff.

🧑‍💼 Agent assist

Real-time help for the human: suggested answers with sources, next-best action, form filling, compliance reminders and an automatic after-call summary.

📊 Analytics & QA

Every conversation transcribed, summarized, tagged and scored against a QA rubric; trends feed back into knowledge, flows and coaching. See QA & omnichannel.

Design rules that matter

RuleWhy
Deterministic where it must be, generative where it helpsIdentity verification, payments, legally required disclosures and cancellations follow fixed flows. Explaining a policy, troubleshooting and summarizing are where LLMs shine.
Always offer a way outA clear route to a human at any point. Trapping customers in a bot loop destroys satisfaction faster than anything else.
Ground every answerAnswers come from approved knowledge and the customer's own records, never the model's memory. No source → say so and hand off.
Voice is its own disciplineLatency budgets under about a second per turn, barge-in, short answers, spelling of names and numbers, and DTMF fallback. See voice agent layers.
Measure resolution, not deflectionA contained conversation where the customer calls back tomorrow is a failure. See metrics.
Compliance by channelCall recording consent, AI disclosure rules, outbound calling rules (such as the US TCPA), PCI DSS for card data and data retention limits differ by channel and country.
