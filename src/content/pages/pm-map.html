The core PM concepts, the tools and KPIs, the soft skills, and seven prioritization methods ranked from beginner-friendly to advanced, with a working RICE / ICE calculator.

Main PM concepts

KPIs to track

  • MAU: monthly active users, a measure of engagement
  • Retention rate: how many users come back
  • Churn rate: how many users leave
  • Feature adoption: how many users use a new feature
  • NPS: net promoter score, an indicator of satisfaction

Key soft skills

  • Leadership: take ownership and create clarity when things get messy
  • Negotiation: balance what's wanted against what's possible
  • Problem-solving: break down unexpected problems fast
  • Communication: vision into tasks, pain into priorities, goals into metrics
  • Active listening: insights come from your team and users, not assumptions
