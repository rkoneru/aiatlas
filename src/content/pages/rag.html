Retrieval-Augmented Generation & its math

LLMs augmented with retrieved knowledge. Two pipelines: offline ingestion builds the index, online query retrieves, reranks and composes the prompt at runtime, so answers have fewer hallucinations and cite sources.

Design choices that matter

Chunking

Split on structure (headings, sections), not fixed characters. Keep metadata (source, date, ACL) on every chunk.

Hybrid retrieval

Combine vector similarity with keyword (BM25) search. Exact identifiers like error codes are often missed by embeddings alone.

Rerank & filter

A cross-encoder reranker over the top 20–50 candidates usually beats raising k. Filter by permissions before the LLM sees anything.

Citations

Give chunks IDs in the prompt and require the model to cite them. That makes answers auditable and lets you measure faithfulness.

Evaluate

Measure retrieval (recall@k, MRR) separately from generation (faithfulness, answer relevance). Fix the weaker half first.

Freshness

Incremental re-indexing on document change, plus TTLs for fast-moving sources such as incident notes.

RAG formulas & concepts

The math behind every RAG pipeline: embed, measure similarity, take the top-k, rerank, build context, generate, evaluate. Step through the pipeline, then try each formula on live numbers.

Embeddings & cosine similarity

e = femb(text)   ·   cos(q, d) = q · d‖q‖ ‖d‖

Text becomes a vector that captures meaning. Cosine similarity compares direction, not length: 1 means same direction, 0 unrelated, −1 opposite. Real embeddings have hundreds to thousands of dimensions; this demo uses two.

Chunking

chunks ≈ ⌈ (N − overlap) / (size − overlap) ⌉

Common approaches: fixed size (e.g. 500 tokens), with overlap (e.g. 100 tokens), or by structure (headings and sections). Overlap keeps sentences that straddle a boundary retrievable, at the cost of more vectors.

Top-k retrieval & reranking

Dk = TopKd∈D sim(q, d)   →   s′(q, d) = frerank(q, d)

The bi-encoder scores query and chunk separately, which is fast but coarse. The reranker reads them together, which is slower but sharper, and can reorder the list: watch d₂ overtake d₁.
