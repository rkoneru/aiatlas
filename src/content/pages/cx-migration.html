Customer service AI · Modernization

From IVR & legacy bots to LLM agents

Most organizations already have an IVR menu and an intent-based chatbot. Replacing them in one go is risky; migrating intent by intent, with the old system as a safety net, is not. The plan below moves from logs to shadow mode to live traffic, and the scorer helps you decide what each intent should become.

Intent treatment scorer

Rate an intent on four factors. The result suggests whether it should stay a fixed flow, become an LLM-handled intent with guardrails, or go straight to a person.
