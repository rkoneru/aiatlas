AI value tracking & KPIs

"We launched it" is not value. Track a short chain of metrics for each initiative: adoption (are people using it?), operational (is the work faster or better?), financial (did cost or revenue move?), plus quality and risk (is it safe and accurate?). Measure the baseline before launch, and report the gap.

Attribution: did AI cause the change?

Baseline first

Measure the metric for weeks before launch. Without a baseline, every number is a guess.

Control groups

Roll out to some teams or regions first and compare with those not yet using AI (difference-in-differences).

Count the full cost

Include review time, rework and escalations, not just the minutes the AI saved.
