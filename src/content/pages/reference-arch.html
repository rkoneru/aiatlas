Layer by layer

The AI operating system

A prompt that goes in and a response that comes out breaks on complex tasks. Reliable agents need a runtime around the model, the way programs need an operating system: memory, a planner, a scheduler, a tool router, an evaluator and an interface, connected by a knowledge graph of entities and relationships. The loop becomes plan → act → evaluate → improve. The future isn't only bigger models; it's better systems.
