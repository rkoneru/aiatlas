AI, ML, DL, GenAI, LLMs, RAG & Agentic AI

These concepts are related, not interchangeable. Think of them as a hierarchy that keeps building up: each ring is a subset of the one outside it. RAG and agentic AI are not smaller rings. They are system patterns built on top of LLMs. Click a layer.

Evolution: how these build on each other

Key modern concepts to know

Multimodal AI

Works with text, images, audio, video and more. See VLMs.

RAG

Retrieves knowledge from external sources before generating. See RAG.

Fine-tuning

Customizes models for specific tasks and domains. See LLM optimization.

Prompt engineering

Designing better prompts and context for better outputs.

AI safety

Building trustworthy, safe and responsible AI systems.

AI agents

Autonomous AI that can plan, act and adapt toward goals. See AI agents.

Why this matters: knowing the hierarchy tells you which tool fits the problem. A fraud score needs ML, not an LLM. A policy Q&A bot needs RAG, not fine-tuning. A multi-step task with side effects needs an agent, and guardrails.
