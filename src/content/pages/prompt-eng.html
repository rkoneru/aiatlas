Prompt engineering: the complete guide

A good prompt is a work brief, not a magic phrase. It tells the AI what to produce, who it's for, what to use, which rules to follow, how to structure it and what success looks like. Better prompts aren't longer prompts. They're clearer transfers of intent.

Prompting is iterative

  
Write→Test→Review→Refine→Repeat

  
Your first prompt doesn't need to be perfect. Ask: was the answer relevant? What information was missing? What should I change next time? When it fails, use the prompt debugger. For reusable templates, see the 8 prompt frameworks; for recurring work, turn it into a skill.

Eight acronym templates for writing better prompts. Pick a framework, fill in the fields (each starts with the infographic's example), and copy the finished prompt. They all cover the same few things: context, goal, role, constraints, output format and how to judge success.
