Agent Flow Studio

Design an agentic flow from the Atlas building blocks. The Performance Overview scores it live on 8 dimensions, with one-click fixes and a simulated run.

How it works
  • Click a block on the left to add it. If a block is selected, the new one chains after it.
  • Drag cards to move them. Drag from the dot on a card's bottom edge to another card to connect them, or click the dot and then the target.
  • Click a connection or its label to rename or delete it. Delete removes the selection; Esc cancels.
  • Card chips: inputs · outputs · open issues · estimated latency (ms).
  • The score is the weighted share of the applicable checks that pass. Checks that don't apply are skipped.
