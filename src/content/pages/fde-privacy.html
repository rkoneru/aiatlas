Privacy engineering for AI

Personal data flows through prompts, logs, indexes and evaluation sets. Privacy engineering decides where it is removed, where it is replaced with tokens that can be reversed only by authorized code, where it is stored and for how long, and who can retrieve it.

Try redaction

A simple pattern-based redactor running in your browser. Production systems combine patterns with trained entity recognizers (for example, Microsoft Presidio) and test both on real samples.
