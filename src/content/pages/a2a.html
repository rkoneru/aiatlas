4 · A2A

Agent-to-Agent coordination

MCP connects an agent to tools. A2A connects an agent to other agents, which may be owned by other teams, partners or vendors. Agents publish Agent Cards, get discovered through a registry, and exchange tasks, status events and artifacts through a governed gateway.

Version & governance

Stable

v1.0

A2A reached v1.0, its first stable, production-ready release, in March 2026. Build against 1.x and check the spec's changelog before relying on newer features.

Neutral home

Agentic AI Foundation

Google donated A2A to the Linux Foundation in June 2025. In August 2026 it moved into the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF), the Linux Foundation's home for agent standards, alongside MCP and AGENTS.md. More than 150 organizations backed it at its first anniversary.

Sources: Google Open Source Blog, Linux Foundation, AAIF.
