Agent-to-Agent coordination

MCP connects an agent to tools. A2A connects an agent to other agents, which may be owned by other teams, partners or vendors. Agents publish Agent Cards, get discovered through a registry, and exchange tasks, status events and artifacts through a governed gateway.
