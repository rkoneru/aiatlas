Context retrieval across apps & databases

One request, many stores. Agents rarely find what they need in a single vector index: the answer may sit in a SQL table, a graph, a ticketing app and a wiki at once. A retrieval layer translates the question, routes it to the right stores, writes the right query for each, retrieves, refines and reranks the results, and lets the model ask for more if needed. Platforms such as Airweave sync many apps and databases into one searchable layer for agents.
